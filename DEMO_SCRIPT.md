# 🎥 TalentScout AI 

---

> "Every recruiter faces the same bottleneck: they get a job description and dozens of profiles. They spend *days* manually reading CVs, shortlisting, sending cold messages — and still don't know who's actually interested before they reach out."

> "Most tools match on keywords. They tell you *who has the skills*. But they can't tell you *who will respond*. That gap costs hiring teams weeks."

> "We built TalentScout AI to solve both halves of this problem — at once."

Step five: Final score — 70% match, 30% interest — and the ranking is complete."
